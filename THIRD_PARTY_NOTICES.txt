4. wordfreq English word frequencies (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq)
==============================================================================

The VR keyboard's word suggestions use a table of the 30,000 most frequent English
words and their frequencies, made from the wordfreq data (version 3.1.1) by
build/gen_kb_words.py: the words kept as they are, their frequencies rounded to
1/32 of a decade, packed. That table is an adaptation of the wordfreq data and is
shared under the same license.

wordfreq data: Copyright (c) Robyn Speer and the wordfreq contributors, built from
sources credited in the wordfreq project. Licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

5. NVIDIA NeMo-Speech.cpp (https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo-Speech.cpp)
==============================================================================

Speech recognition (the ASR pipeline, its ggml runtime and its C interface) is compiled
into LegendPanel from NeMo-Speech.cpp at revision e790e463b2913cff9f989a3df224c587f14b4c5c
(version 0.3.0), unchanged (third_party/nemo_speech; LegendPanel adds its own build
files and a stand-in for the SentencePiece header, whose word-boosting use is not built).

9. NVIDIA Nemotron Speech Streaming 0.6B (the speech model; downloaded, not included)
==============================================================================

On the first use of voice typing with Nemotron, LegendPanel downloads
nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b.q8_0.gguf from NVIDIA's own Hugging Face repository
(https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b, revision
ebe59e5a817142986528bbbee5dba8db7b38ed50) into %LOCALAPPDATA%\LegendEye\models and
checks its size and SHA-256 before use. The model is governed by the NVIDIA Open Model
License (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/);
by using it you accept that license. LegendPanel does not include or modify the model.

10. Silero VAD v6.2.0 (downloaded, not included)
==============================================================================

For hands-free voice typing LegendPanel downloads ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin (Silero VAD,
https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad, in whisper.cpp's file format) from
https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad (revision
9ffd54a1e1ee413ddf265af9913beaf518d1639b), checks its SHA-256 and rewrites it as the
GGUF file NeMo-Speech.cpp reads (the same steps as NeMo-Speech.cpp's converter).
Silero VAD: Copyright (c) 2020-present Silero Team; MIT License. The whisper.cpp file:
Copyright (c) 2023-2026 The ggml authors; MIT License (texts as in 6 above).
