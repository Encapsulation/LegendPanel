## Screens and docking
- Dock screens, the keyboard and the panel into assemblies that hinge at every joint. Grip the handle on a joint and push or pull, or use the stick, and both sides fold together.
- Lock a window's outer edge (its bar > More > Lock edge) and the joint bends both sides while that edge stays put and everything stays flush.
- One-hand corner resize, by laser or by gripping the corner. Docked screens stay centered on their parent while they resize.
- A docked row bends on one continuous curve; a screen above or below takes its parent's curve, or keeps its own with Follow off.
- Snap screens, the keyboard and the panel to body trackers. Pick which body parts count: waist, chest, feet, knees, arms or others.
- Auto-tile, saved layouts, Make Parent, Hide group, Recenter and voice typing on every screen's bar.
- The Screens settings pick which screen you're changing, by its window name.
- Restore minimized: a window that's minimized when its screen opens comes back quietly, so it never sits on Loading. A window you minimize stays down.

## The wristwatch
- A watch on your wrist with six looks: Chronograph, Sport, Smart, Diver, Mini panel and Band.
- Time and date, FPS with the last frames, CPU, GPU and VRAM use, batteries and what's playing, drawn at your headset's refresh rate.
- Buttons for the panel, the keyboard, your screens, previous, play / pause and next, plus a lock. The laser's selection snaps to the nearest button, and each button shows its name.
- Grab it to move it: near a wrist it goes on that wrist, anywhere else it stays in the room. Size it with the stick, and bend its strap around your wrist.

## Binds
- Every bind in one place: Binds in the menu, in VR and on the desktop, with tabs for All, LegendPanel, Game mode, Key binds and Pictures.
- Game mode buttons can send any key, any mouse button, the wheel, media keys or one of your action buttons and macros, picked from grouped submenus.
- Key binds: hold controller buttons (or a mouse side button) to hold any key or key combo.
- Binds never clash: buttons LegendPanel already uses can't be taken by a key bind, and the other way round. In Game mode, a key bind you hold wins over that button's game bind.
- Learn: press a controller button and its bind opens. Record any combo.

## Aim, click and grab
- Right-click from the trackpad (tap low), middle-click (tap the top), and scroll with the trackpad.
- Trigger-pressure stabilization: the ray steadies as you squeeze, so the click lands where you aimed.
- Button snap: the selection is pulled to the nearest button.
- Kinetic physics: throw panels and screens and they fly on with momentum, docking onto what they reach.
- Lock the panel, the keyboard, each screen and the watch in place.

## Keyboard and voice
- Press to keystroke in 5.4 ms (median), against 22.7 ms for SteamVR's keyboard, measured on the same test rig.
- A Lock key in the keyboard's top bar, gaze fade, and a keyboard button that can close or recenter it.
- Voice typing runs locally on your GPU with NVIDIA Nemotron. One voice mode for the keyboard's mic key and every screen's voice button: hold to talk, tap or hold, or toggle (hands-free).

## Eyes and face (with LegendEye)
- Eyes + Face in the menu: eye control of the panel, the eye cursor, calibration, foveated rendering, and LegendEye's tracking: pause, start and recenter.
- Face tracking on or off, expressions, and face smoothing, synced both ways with LegendEye.

## The menu
- Auto menu: with eye control off, the panel's Menu button opens the full desktop-style menu in VR, bigger and easier to hit. With eye control on, it opens the eye menu.
- Clearer groups and names, a Back on every submenu, color-coded tabs, tooltips on everything, and pictures of each watch style.
- Summon brings the panel to you, undocked, or the watch back to your wrist: your choice.

## Playspace
- 6DOF orient: tilt, turn and roll your whole playspace with a chord, snap back with another.
- Teleport: hold, aim the arc, let go. Glide or jump, or one button for teleport and drag.
- Space drag with momentum, friction and gravity, and stick turn.

## Games
- Game mode per screen: while your laser is on it, your controllers become an Xbox controller for that game, and your VR game stops seeing them. Its button picks the style: your own binds (every button bindable to keys, mouse or macros) for PC apps, or a plain controller with the standard layout and analog triggers for emulators. Sticks as gamepad does it all the time. Both need the free ViGEmBus driver.
