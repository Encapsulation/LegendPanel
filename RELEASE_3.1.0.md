# LegendPanel 3.1.0

## Show when
- One place for when things show: Panel > Show when, with a page each for the panel, the watch, screens and the keyboard.
- Each one picks Always, Head turn (shows while your head points at it) or Eye look (with LegendEye; facing it with your head works too).
- Look angle: how far off it you can look and still see it. Stays: how long it stays after you look away.
- Faint when away: it stays as a faint ghost you can still find, or disappears completely.
- The watch can now hide until you look at your wrist, like the panel. It must face you, so it stays hidden while your arm is down. Pointing at it or holding it keeps it shown.
- A screen's or the keyboard's Gaze fade button now opens its Show when page. Your old Gaze fade settings carry over unchanged.

## Cable turns
- Counts how many times you have turned round one way, so your headset cable never tangles.
- No drift: it counts from the headset's own tracking, so space drag, playspace turns and recentering never change the count. Tested through 40 full turns with nodding and tilting, ending exact.
- Shows on the watch (a tag at the top) and on the panel (a Cable chip), with the way to unwind and the number of turns: green, amber past your limit, red a turn later.
- Shows from 1.5 turns by default and hides again once you are nearly unwound. Pick any number of turns, or always.
- Cable is straight: click the tag on the watch, the Cable chip on the panel, or the button in the menu.
- Floor ring (optional): a ring on the floor under you that fills as you wind up.
- Off by default on wireless headsets (Virtual Desktop, ALVR, Steam Link); turn it on anyway if you like.
- Panel > Show when > Cable turns.
